at process scope and draws its SMuFL outlines at a 40-point em / 10-point
staff-space size. Staff lines, stems and ledger lines are drawing primitives.
The same registration also makes Font.custom("Bravura", fixedSize:) available.

Run python3 scripts/fetch-notation-font.py to prepare the build input, or
add --check to verify local files without networking. The OTF is excluded
from Git; the fetch script, source notice and license are tracked.

QinXu's notation remains a practice view. PianoScore does not preserve the
original beaming, voice directions, rests, key signature or tie layout.
Unequal durations are not merged into one stem. Nonstandard durations keep
an explicit beat-count annotation; this does not claim full engraving fidelity.
